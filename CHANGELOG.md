# Changelog

## 0.20.01 - Beta (first public release)

### Guides
- 174 guides covering 2,361 quests.
- Starter zones for all ten races.
- Classic 1-60 for Alliance and Horde, one folder per zone with its level range.
- Dungeon and raid guides, all professions, mage class quests, pets and mounts.
- OctoWoW custom zones and quests.
- Chapters chain into each other, finished quests are skipped, quests your race or class cannot take are removed.

### Viewer and arrow
- Step viewer with goal rows, progress bar, tabs, resize, lock and transparency.
- Live waypoint arrow with distance and travel time, including cross-zone targets.
- Auto-accept, auto turn-in and an optional accept-everything mode.

### Gold, items and events
- Gold tracker with a pie chart and a detailed report, level tracker.
- Built-in vendor prices for 19,663 items, learned and Auction House prices, sell advice in tooltips.
- Best quest reward picker for your spec, Gear Finder.
- Events page with list and calendar views.

### Help
- `/oghelp` opens an in-game command list.
