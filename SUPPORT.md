# Getting help

## Fastest answers
1. Type **`/oghelp`** in game for every command.
2. Read the [FAQ](README.md#faq).
3. Ask in **[KyDa's Discord](https://discord.gg/v4xRSyqRJy)** or the **[OctoWoW Discord](https://discord.gg/naMJsBfpYt)**.

## Report a bug or a wrong step
[Open an issue](https://github.com/KyDaog/OctoGuide/issues/new/choose) and pick the matching form. The more of these you include, the faster it gets fixed:

- OctoGuide version (shown in the title of `/oghelp`'s chat line, or in the addon list)
- Faction, race, class and level
- The guide name and step number
- What the step said and what actually happened
- For a wrong NPC position: stand where the NPC really is, type **`/og here`** and paste the line it prints
- For red error text: copy it word for word

## Arrow trouble
- Two arrows on screen: `/pfquest arrow` turns off pfQuest's arrow.
- Arrow points the wrong way: `/og arrow flip`.
- Arrow stuck on an old target: `/og clear`.

## Updating
Close the game, delete the old `Interface\AddOns\OctoGuide` folder, copy in the new one, restart the client. Your settings and progress are kept in your `WTF` folder.
